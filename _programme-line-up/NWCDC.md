<p>The Community Development Councils (CDCs) were established to build a
tightly knit, compassionate and self-reliant community. <strong>North West CDC</strong> works
closely with various community and corporate partners to strengthen Singapore’s
social fabric; and encourages the more able and successful to come forward
to help the less successful, and to foster greater sense of community ownership
and self-help among the residents.</p>
<p>We strive to align our activities and efforts with our mission:</p>
<p>(a) <strong>Aggregator of Needs and Resources</strong>
<br>To help the less fortunate through local assistance programmes</p>
<p>(b) <strong>Builder of Capabilities and Capacities</strong>
<br>To bond the community through projects &amp; programmes to bring them
closer to one another</p>
<p>(c) <strong>Connector of Communities</strong>
<br>To develop strategic partnerships with corporate and community partners
to better serve our residents</p>
<p>To achieve our mission, North West CDC continues to build programmes to
provide support to residents under our five community pillars:</p>
<p><strong>i. Caring Communities</strong>
<br>Encourage a giving culture and uplift the lives of residents through social
support programmes and networks</p>
<p><strong>ii. Healthy Communities</strong>
<br>Promote healthy lifestyle, encourage residents to bond and take charge
of their own health</p>
<p><strong>iii. Inclusive Communities</strong>
<br>Strengthen social fabric by embracing and involving different segments
of society</p>
<p><strong>iv. Learning Communities</strong>
<br>Promote lifelong learning mindset for personal and professional development</p>
<p><strong>v. Sustainable Communities</strong>
<br>Cultivate an eco-friendly lifestyle and building a green environment for
our future generations</p>
